Stay in the loop

Our newsletter goes out to educators, owners, operators and directors across British Columbia. Funding changes, regulatory updates, wage news, and what we're pushing for next.

What you'll get

No noise

We send when there is something worth sending — a funding announcement that changes how you budget, a regulatory interpretation that affects your licence, a wage decision, or an open call for input.

Your email address is used only to send you the newsletter. We don't sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone else. Every issue carries an unsubscribe link, and you can also unsubscribe any time by emailing CCPofBC@gmail.com.

Interested in doing more than reading? We take volunteers for advisory and board roles. Email us to ask what's open.
