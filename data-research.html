A pathway to universal child care in BC by 2028

Eight years after $10 a Day was announced as universal, it reaches about one child in ten. Budget 2026 paused new enrolment for a three-year stabilization period. We argue that pause is the moment to fix the funding model rather than wait it out.

Stabilize

From April 2027, pay operating and fee reduction funding on 75% of licensed capacity rather than enrolment — so programs survive the current enrolment trough with their educators still on staff, instead of laying them off.

Include

Three changes, none of them needing new legislation, so that enrolling a child with support needs brings the staffing to support them in the classroom: a standard of practice, a support supplement paid to the provider, and an accessibility grant.

Go universal

From April 2028, fund every licensed centre at its published regional benchmark, less a single province-wide parent co-pay. Costed in the report at roughly $1.2 to $1.9 billion a year, depending on the co-pay and occupancy.

B.C. Child Care Sector Occupational Competencies

The competencies behind ECE certification in this province — what an educator is expected to understand, value and be able to do. Knowledge specifications, a values and ethics section carrying the ECEBC Code of Ethics, and eight functional areas from maintaining a safe environment through to advocating for the child care system. Used for self-assessment, staff evaluation, professional development planning, and curriculum and practicum work.

Published by the Province of British Columbia, ECE Registry, updated 2026. 27 pages. Also on the Province’s ECE profession page, under Legal requirements.
